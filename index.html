Reaction Test measures how quickly you respond to a visual signal, giving you a result in milliseconds.

ABOUT THE TEST

A test of visual reaction time.

Reaction time is the amount of time it takes to respond after noticing a stimulus. In this test, the stimulus is a screen changing from red to green.

Your score represents the time between the moment the screen turns green and the moment you click. The result is measured in milliseconds (ms).

HOW IT WORKS
01

Wait for green.

The test begins with a red screen. After a random delay, the screen changes to green. Stay focused and wait for the signal.

02

React.

Click as soon as the screen turns green. The test records the time between the green signal and your click.

03

Get your result.

Your reaction time is displayed immediately. Lower numbers mean you reacted faster.

UNDERSTANDING YOUR SCORE

What does your time mean?

Reaction times vary from person to person and can be affected by factors such as attention, familiarity with the test, and your input device.

ACCURACY

Why can your score change?

Reaction time is not always the same. Your attention, timing, surroundings, and input device can all affect the result.

For the most consistent results, use the same device and input method when comparing your scores.

LEADERBOARD

See how you compare.

You can save your result with a username and add it to the global leaderboard. Scores are ranked from the fastest reaction time to the slowest.

No account is required. You can simply enter a username when you choose to save a score.

TIPS

Want a more consistent result?

  • Focus on the center of the screen.
  • Keep your cursor ready before starting.
  • Don't click before the screen turns green.
  • Use the same device when comparing results.
  • Try several rounds instead of relying on one score.
